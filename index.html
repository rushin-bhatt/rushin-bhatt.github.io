About Me

Hi! I'm Rushin, and my journey with AI and Machine Learning has been all about finding connections between disciplines, industries, and even art forms. As a Machine Learning intern at a fast paced startup in Mumbai named Fynd, I built an AI driven product catalog enrichment pipeline that showed how large scale machine learning and agentic AI can transform something as dynamic as retail. The beauty of this role was that I had the opportunity to work on the product side as well, directly under the CTPO, a blend of tech and product.

I'm also a pianist, and I've completed all levels of the Trinity College of Music Classical Piano and Theory music examinations. What excites me most is extending these ideas into creative spaces. I've been experimenting with how AI agents can augment music composition and performance, blending my background as a pianist with my academic interests.

I'm in my last semester at Columbia in New York City. Last summer I interned at Spot AI, Inc. as a Forward Deployed Engineer, working directly with customers on video agent deployment.
